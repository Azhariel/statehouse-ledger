The '''governor of Kansas''' is the [[head of state]] of [[Kansas]]{{sfn|Kan. Const.|loc=[https://50constitutions.org/ks/constitution/section-id-64239 art. I, § 3]}} and the commander-in-chief of the state's [[Kansas National Guard|military forces]].{{sfn|Kan. Const.|loc=[https://50constitutions.org/ks/constitution/section-id-64431 art. VIII, § 4]}} The governor has a duty to enforce state laws,{{sfn|Kan. Const.|loc=[https://50constitutions.org/ks/constitution/section-id-64239 art. I, § 3]}} and the power to either approve or veto bills passed by the [[Kansas Legislature]],{{sfn|Kan. Const.|loc=[https://50constitutions.org/ks/constitution/section-id-64296 art. II, § 14]}} to convene the legislature at any time,{{sfn|Kan. Const.|loc=[https://50constitutions.org/ks/constitution/section-id-64243 art. I, § 5]}} and to grant [[pardon]]s.{{sfn|Kan. Const.|loc=[https://50constitutions.org/ks/constitution/section-id-64247 art. I, § 7]}}

Since becoming a state, Kansas has had 48 governors. The state's longest-serving governors were [[Robert Docking]], [[John W. Carlin]], and [[Bill Graves]], each of whom served 8 years (Docking served four two-year terms; Carlin and Graves each served 2 4-year terms). The shortest-serving governor was [[John McCuish]], who served only 11 days after the resignation of [[Fred Hall]].

The current governor is [[Kansas Democratic Party|Democrat]] [[Laura Kelly]], who took office on January 14, 2019.

== List of governors ==

=== Kansas Territory ===
[[Kansas Territory]] was organized on May 30, 1854, from land that had previously been part of [[Missouri Territory]].<ref>{{usstat|10|277}}</ref> Despite existing only for six years, it had six governors appointed by the [[president of the United States]].

=== State of Kansas ===
[[File:Standard of the Governor of Kansas (pre-1961).svg|thumb|right|150px|[[Flags of governors of the U.S. states|Flag of the governor]] prior to 1961. It is unclear when the governor's flag was first created]]
The eastern bulk of Kansas Territory was [[Admission to the Union|admitted to the Union]] as Kansas on January 29, 1861.<ref>{{usstat|12|126}}</ref> The [[Wyandotte Constitution|Kansas Constitution]] provided that a governor and [[Lieutenant Governor of Kansas|lieutenant governor]] be elected every 2 years, for a term commencing on the second Monday in the January after the election.{{sfn|Thorpe|1909|p=1243|loc=1861 art. I, § 1}} An amendment in 1972 increased terms to four years, with a limit that a governor could not serve more than two terms in a row, and provided that the governor and lieutenant governor are elected on the same [[ticket (election)|ticket]].<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.newspapers.com/article/the-wichita-eagle-kansans-ok-3-amendment/209049434/ |title=Kansans OK 3 Amendments to Constitution |date=November 8, 1972 |page=13A |last=Gregory |first=Jim |newspaper=[[The Wichita Eagle]] |via=Newspapers.com}}</ref> In the original constitution, should the office of governor be vacant, the powers would devolve upon the lieutenant governor, who nonetheless would remain in that office;{{sfn|Thorpe|1909|p=1244|loc=1861 art. I, § 11}} the 1972 amendment changed it so that, in such an event, the lieutenant governor becomes governor.{{sfn|Kan. Const.|loc=[https://50constitutions.org/ks/constitution/section-id-64251 art. I, § 11]}}

Until 2018, there was no age or residency requirement to run for the office; in 2017, three teenagers were doing so.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Woodall |first=Hunter |date=September 28, 2017 |title=As third teen joins Kansas governor race, consider this: No rule says a dog can't run |newspaper=[[The Kansas City Star]] |url=http://www.kansascity.com/news/politics-government/article175956836.html |access-date=October 26, 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170928205105/http://www.kansascity.com/news/politics-government/article175956836.html |archive-date=September 28, 2017 |url-status=live}}</ref> In 2018, a law was passed establishing the age to run for governor and lieutenant governor at 25.<ref>{{Cite news |date=May 18, 2018 |title=New law sets minimum age for Kansas governor candidates |website=[[KWCH-DT]] |url=https://www.kwch.com/content/news/New-law-requires-Kansas-governor-candidates-to-be-at-least-25-years-old-483075941.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180522020801/https://www.kwch.com/content/news/New-law-requires-Kansas-governor-candidates-to-be-at-least-25-years-old-483075941.html |archive-date=May 22, 2018 |url-status=live}}</ref>
