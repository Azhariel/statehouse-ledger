The [[governor of South Dakota]] is the [[head of government]] of the [[U.S. state]] of [[South Dakota]]. The current governor is [[Larry Rhoden]], representing the [[South Dakota Republican Party|Republican Party]], serving since 2025.

==Governors==
{{for|governors before statehood|List of governors of Dakota Territory}}
[[Dakota Territory]] was organized on March 2, 1861;<ref>{{usstat|12|239}}</ref> on November 2, 1889, it was split into the states of [[North Dakota]] and South Dakota.<ref>{{usstat|25|676}}</ref>

The [[Constitution of South Dakota]] originally provided for the election of a governor and lieutenant governor every two years with no limits. A law passed in 1947 prohibited parties from nominating someone who had served two consecutive terms, effectively creating a term limit, and an amendment in 1972 increased term lengths to four years while formally prohibiting someone from serving three consecutive terms. Should the office of governor become vacant, the lieutenant governor becomes governor.{{sfn|Kallenbach|1977|pp=542–544}}

The Republican Party has been in control of the governorship continuously since 1979, Democrats last won the governorship of South Dakota in 1974. This is the longest drought of any state where the opposite party last held the office.
