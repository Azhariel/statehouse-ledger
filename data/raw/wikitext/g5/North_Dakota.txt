The [[governor of North Dakota]] is the [[head of government]] of the [[U.S. state]] of [[North Dakota]]. The [[Governor (United States)|governor]] is the head of the [[Executive (government)|executive branch]] of [[Government of North Dakota|North Dakota's state government]] and is charged with enforcing state laws.
 
There have been 32 governors since North Dakota became a state, serving 33 distinct terms, with [[William Langer]] having been elected to multiple terms. The current officeholder is [[North Dakota Republican Party|Republican]] [[Kelly Armstrong]].

==Governors==
{{for|governors before statehood|List of governors of Dakota Territory}}
[[Dakota Territory]] was organized on March 2, 1861;<ref>{{usstat|12|239}}</ref> on November 2, 1889, it was split into the states of North Dakota and [[South Dakota]].<ref>{{usstat|25|676}}</ref>

The [[Constitution of North Dakota]] originally provided for the election of a governor and lieutenant governor every two years, which was changed to four years in 1964.<ref>{{Cite web |title=North Dakota State and County Official Term Lengths, Initiative 5 (1964) |url=https://ballotpedia.org/North_Dakota_State_and_County_Official_Term_Lengths,_Initiative_5_(1964) |access-date=May 30, 2023}}</ref> A limit of two terms was added in 2023.<ref>{{Cite web |title=North Dakota Constitutional Measure 1, Term Limits for Governor and State Legislators Initiative (2022) |url=https://ballotpedia.org/North_Dakota_Constitutional_Measure_1,_Term_Limits_for_Governor_and_State_Legislators_Initiative_(2022) |access-date=May 30, 2023}}</ref> The governor and lieutenant governor are elected together on a [[ticket (election)|ticket]],<ref>ND Const. art. V, § 3</ref> and should the office of governor become vacant, the lieutenant governor becomes governor.<ref>ND Const. art. V, § 11</ref> The term of office begins on the December 15 following the election.<ref>ND Const. art. V, § 5</ref>
