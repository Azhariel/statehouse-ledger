==Partisan identification in the electorate==
As of July 2025, Arizona's registered voters include 1,597,705 Republicans (35.7%), 1,270,307 Democrats (28.4%), 31,905 Libertarians (0.7%), 39,804 No Labels (0.9%), 5,045 Greens (0.1%), and 1,525,855 "Other" (34.1%).<ref>{{Cite web |title=July 2025 - Voter Registration Statistics {{!}} Arizona Secretary of State |url=https://azsos.gov/elections/election-information/voter-registration-statistics |access-date=2025-04-16 |website=azsos.gov}}</ref>

==State politics==
Most political offices are currently held by members of the Republican Party. Both U.S. Senators and 3 of out the 9 House of Representatives members are Democrats, (the other six are Republicans) Many were first elected in [[2020 Arizona elections|the 2018 elections]].
