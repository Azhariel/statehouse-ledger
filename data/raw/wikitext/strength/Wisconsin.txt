{{Short description|Politics in the US state of Wisconsin}}
The following tables indicate the historic party affiliation of elected officials in the [[U.S. state]] of [[Wisconsin]], including: [[Governor of Wisconsin|Governor]], [[Lieutenant Governor of Wisconsin|Lieutenant Governor]], [[Secretary of State of Wisconsin|Secretary of State]], [[Attorney General of Wisconsin|Attorney General]], [[State Treasurer of Wisconsin|State Treasurer]], [[Superintendent of Public Instruction of Wisconsin|Superintendent of Public Instruction]]. The tables also indicate the historical party composition in the [[Wisconsin State Senate|State Senate]],<ref name=2005bb>Wisconsin Legislative Reference Bureau (comp.). ''[https://docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/misc/lrb/blue_book/2005_2006/500_legislative.pdf State of Wisconsin 2005-2006 Blue Book]''. Madison: Wisconsin Legislature Joint Committee on Legislative Organization, 2005, p. 271.</ref> [[Wisconsin State Assembly|State Assembly]],<ref name=2005bb/> the [[List of United States senators from Wisconsin|State delegation to the United States Senate]], and the [[List of United States representatives from Wisconsin|State delegation to the United States House of Representatives]]. For years in which a [[United States presidential election]] was held, the tables indicate which party's nominees received the state's electoral votes.
