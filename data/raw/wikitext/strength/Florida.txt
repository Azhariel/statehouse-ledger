{{Politics of Florida}}

The state of [[Florida]] has historically been dominated by the [[Democratic Party (United States)|Democratic party]], with a brief period of [[Republican Party (United States)|Republican]] officeholders during [[Reconstruction era|Reconstruction]], before becoming a core state of the [[Solid South]]. A dramatic reversal began in the late 20th century, with the 21st century having been dominated by Republicans.<ref>{{cite web|last=Bowman|first=Meghan|date=November 7, 2025|title=Red, blue, or neither? The changing color of Florida politics|url=https://www.wusf.org/politics-issues/2025-11-07/red-blue-neither-changing-color-florida-politics|website=wusf|access-date=May 30, 2026}}</ref>

==Partisan affiliation of elected officials==
The following tables indicate party affiliation in the [[U.S. state]] of [[Florida]] for the individual elected offices of: 
*[[List of governors of Florida|Governor]]
*[[Lieutenant Governor of Florida|Lieutenant Governor]]
*[[Florida Attorney General|Attorney General]]
*[[Chief Financial Officer of Florida|Chief Financial Officer]]
*[[Florida Commissioner of Agriculture|Commissioner of Agriculture]]

As well as the following historical offices that were elected from 1889 to 2005:
*[[Secretary of State of Florida|Secretary of State]]
*[[Florida Comptroller|Comptroller]]
*[[Treasurer, Insurance Commissioner, and Fire Marshal of Florida|Treasurer/Insurance Commissioner/Fire Marshal]]
*[[Florida Department of Education|Commissioner of Education]] (called the Superintendent of Public Instruction before 1969)
The table also indicates the historical party composition in the:
*[[Florida Senate|State Senate]]
*[[Florida House of Representatives|State House of Representatives]]
*[[List of United States senators from Florida|State delegation to the U.S. Senate]] (individually)
*[[List of United States representatives from Florida|State delegation to the U.S. House of Representatives]]

For years in which a [[United States presidential election|presidential election]] was held, the table indicates which party's nominees received the state's electoral votes. For the [[American Civil War|Civil War]] years, the table indicates the state's delegation to the [[Confederate States Congress|Confederate Congress]], in lieu of the [[United States Congress|U.S. Congress]].
