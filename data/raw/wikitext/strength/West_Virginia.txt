The following tables indicate the historic party affiliation of elected officials in the [[U.S. state]] of [[West Virginia]], including: [[List of governors of West Virginia|governor]], [[Secretary of State of West Virginia|secretary of state]], [[List of attorneys general of West Virginia|attorney general]], state auditor,<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.wvculture.org/history/stateauditor.html |title=West Virginia State Auditors |publisher=WVCulture.org |access-date=2011-01-03 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120929081530/http://www.wvculture.org/history/stateauditor.html |archive-date=2012-09-29 |url-status=dead}}</ref> state treasurer,<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.wvculture.org/history/statetreasurer.html |title=West Virginia State Treasurers |publisher=WVCulture.org |access-date=2011-01-03 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111116220909/http://www.wvculture.org/history/statetreasurer.html |archive-date=2011-11-16 |url-status=dead}}</ref> and state agriculture commissioner. The tables also indicate the historical party composition in the [[West Virginia Senate|state Senate]], [[West Virginia House of Delegates|state House of Delegates]],<ref>[http://www.legis.state.wv.us/Educational/Publications/Manual_PDF/24-Political_Composition.pdf Educational Publications Manual], State of West Virginia Legislature</ref> [[List of justices of the Supreme Court of Appeals of West Virginia|Supreme Court of Appeals]], [[List of United States senators from West Virginia|delegation to the U.S. Senate]], and the [[List of United States representatives from West Virginia|delegation to the U.S. House of Representatives]]. For years in which a [[United States presidential election|presidential election]] was held, the tables indicate which party's nominees received the state's electoral votes.
